How to play
  1. Take turns pulling the slot handle.
  2. Cash prizes: $300, $400, $500, $600, or $900. A correct consonant earns that amount per match.
  3. Each cash prize and Gamble It All has a 12/84 chance. Miss Turn is 7/84; Big Win is 5/84.
  4. Miss Turn skips your go. Big Win adds $1,500. Gamble It All doubles your score.
  5. After a cash spin, pick one consonant or buy one vowel for $250. Vowel cost applies even if it is absent.
  6. After a correct letter, make one phrase guess. First to $10,000 wins. There are three rounds.
  7. Buzz once per round to guess instantly, even off-turn. A miss uses your buzz.
